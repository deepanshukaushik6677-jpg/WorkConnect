Questions

Designed to feel simple even when the system underneath is serious.

Progressive disclosure keeps complicated workflows out of the user's way until they are relevant.

Can someone hire for a single small task?

Yes. The job model supports one-time work as well as temporary contracts and regular or permanent employment.

Can workers control who contacts them?

Yes. Contact preferences, request permissions, block/report controls and job-gated communication are part of the experience.

Will online payment be required?

No. The initial product can support cash settlement while keeping payment records and later online-payment integration in the architecture.

What can an administrator control?

Users, categories, skills, worker profiles, jobs, applications, hiring relationships, reviews, verification, complaints, bans, settings and basic analytics.
